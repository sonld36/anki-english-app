- (decision) The signal that it is the user's turn is the mic button becoming enabled; it is disabled at all other times. No separate turn indicator
- (decision) Mic button keeps its current placement and form: 80px circle at the bottom of the screen (globals.css .voice-button)

- (assumption) CONFLICT to resolve: green #10b981 is currently the semantic success colour. Making green the brand colour collides with using green to mean Đạt

- (decision) Timer counts UP, not down. It is a measure, not an ultimatum — no time limit on a turn, nothing happens at any elapsed value. Confirms the PRD carries no time-limit rule
- (event) Rendered .working/color-themes-1.html — five soft-green directions on the same practice-screen moment (system turn, hint chip, user turn, inline score with commute passing and deadline failing on final /d/, count-up timer, tap-tap mic). Variations differ in register and in how Đạt is signalled: saturation contrast, light mode, amber reward, shape-only, high-contrast green
- (decision) Dual theme confirmed. LIGHT mode = direction 2 'Sương sớm' (bg #f4f8f2, surface #ffffff, chrome #6f9e77, success #1f9d55, danger #d94f5c). DARK mode = direction 5 'Xanh đậm' (bg #0a120d, surface #122018, chrome #22c55e, success #4ade80, danger #fb7185)
- (decision) Both chosen directions signal Đạt with saturated green rather than a separate reward colour — the amber and shape-only approaches were not picked
- (assumption) OPEN: how the theme is selected — follow system preference, manual toggle, or time of day. Matters because UJ-1 puts Son on the app at 5:30am
- (assumption) CONTRAST PROBLEM: light-mode success #1f9d55 on white is about 3.4:1, below the WCAG AA 4.5:1 floor for normal text. Passes only as large or bold text. Needs darkening or restriction to bold use
- (assumption) REGISTER MISMATCH to check with user: direction 2 reads calm and clean, direction 5 reads high-energy. The product would feel different depending on theme
- (decision) Theme is chosen by an explicit in-app toggle (surfaced in settings), not by system preference and not automatically by time of day
- (change) Light-mode palette darkened to clear WCAG AA 4.5:1 on white — success #1f9d55 (3.49:1) becomes #15803d (5.02:1); danger #d94f5c (4.02:1) becomes #be123c (6.29:1); chrome text #6f9e77 (3.07:1) becomes #4e7355 (5.37:1). Two failures beyond the one originally spotted
- (event) Dark palette verified as-is: success #4ade80 at 9.90:1, danger #fb7185 at 6.41:1, chrome #22c55e at 7.57:1 on surface #0f1e15. No change needed
- (assumption) Register difference between the two themes KEPT — light reads calm, dark reads focused. Read from the user's 'ok'; flagged for correction if that was not the intent
- (decision) Voice is a COACH — encouraging, finds the specific thing that went right, names the specific thing to fix. Not a cold measuring instrument, not a generic cheerleader
- (decision) The app addresses the user as 'Bạn'. Second person, familiar but not slangy
- (decision) When a result is genuinely bad, the voice DROPS the encouragement and goes direct with an instruction — 'Câu này chưa nhận ra được. Nói chậm và to hơn thử xem.' Never fake proximity to success; a coach that praises a bad attempt destroys trust in every later praise. Also never blame the microphone to save face
- (decision) Six sample microcopy lines accepted without changes — first-time production, faster-than-last-time, not-yet with retries left, third-failure reveal, session summary, empty due list
- (decision) No persistent history surface. The transcript exists only until the session ends; after the summary it is gone. The existing HistoryPanel is dropped
- (decision) Long-term progress view deferred out of v1. Progress is visible only as 'vs the previous session' inside the summary
- (decision) Session length is chosen by the user: they pick how many words to use, and the script is generated to match. Answers PRD open question 5 — no fixed turn count
- (assumption) CONFLICT: dropping the transcript removes 'listen to yourself two weeks ago', which the brief recorded as a zero-cost motivation feature, and contradicts story 2.2's 'bản ghi giữ trên máy để đối chiếu về sau'. Within a session, replay still works
- (assumption) CONSEQUENCE: with the long-term view deferred, the TTFW trend — the product's stated north-star and its strongest retention argument — is not visible to users in v1. PRD open question 3 about charting sparse TTFW is moot until that surface exists
- (assumption) The word-count control needs a home on the topic/level screen, and interacts with the 20 due-card cap in FR-20
- (decision) Word count is chosen on the topic/level screen, before the script is generated. Default 5-7 cards
- (decision) When fewer cards are due than the user picked, practise only the due ones — never pad with words that are not due. Protects the spacing schedule from being diluted
- (assumption) Reading of the above: the picker is capped at the number actually due, rather than letting the user pick a number the system then silently overrides
- (event) Rendered .working/key-screen-reveal.html — three treatments of the third-failure reveal in the chosen dark palette. A: answer arrives as another coach chat bubble, minimal ceremony. B: answer breaks out into a distinct ĐÁP ÁN card, session pauses a beat. C: answer plus a targeted micro-lesson on the specific phoneme with a slow-playback control
- (decision) Third-failure reveal uses treatment C — the correct sentence plus a targeted micro-lesson naming the specific phoneme that failed, with an articulation tip. The failure is turned into a small lesson rather than just shown and passed over
- (decision) Slow 0.6x playback dropped from treatment C. Model audio plays at normal speed only
- (assumption) NEW WORK ITEM this creates: a fixed library of per-phoneme articulation tips for Vietnamese speakers, roughly 15-20 entries keyed by phoneme. Should be hand-written and shipped with the app, NOT generated on demand — the same principle already applied to hints, since the user is stuck and frustrated at exactly that moment and must not wait on a network call. Story 2.5 needs updating to include the micro-lesson display
- (decision) Typography delegated to me by the user. Single family: Inter — already in use, strong Vietnamese diacritics, neutral enough to stay out of the content's way. English target sentences are NOT a second family, just larger and heavier
- (decision) Type scale: 12px meta, 14px UI and coach copy, 16px chat bubbles, 20px the revealed target sentence, 24px screen titles. Weights 400/600/700 only
- (decision) Tabular figures for the timer and scores — proportional digits make the count-up timer jitter sideways, right where the user is staring
- (assumption) ISSUE: Inter loaded from Google Fonts covers latin and latin-ext only. IPA Extensions (θ ð ʃ ɪ ə) are NOT in those subsets, so phonetic symbols fall back to the system font and look visibly different from surrounding text. Needs an explicit phonetic font stack so the fallback is deliberate
- (assumption) ISSUE: globals.css line 1 loads Inter via CSS @import, which serialises two network round trips before text renders. next/font/google would self-host and remove both the wait and the font-swap shift
- (decision) Script generation wait uses a NARRATED progress sequence — 'đang chọn từ… đang viết hội thoại… đang thu giọng mẫu…' — not a bare spinner and not progressive entry. Movement makes a 5-15s wait feel shorter
- (decision) Scoring wait shows the user's own utterance immediately; the score fades in underneath a moment later. Gives the user something to read during the ~3s and keeps the conversational rhythm
- (decision) Four remaining states accepted as proposed: Anki-not-running keeps the existing guidance with the AnkiConnect addon code; offline still allows speaking and replay with scoring queued for later, losing no data; denied mic permission explains why it is needed and how to re-enable without nagging; scoring failure says so plainly and never guesses a score, session continues
- (decision) ACCESSIBILITY RULE adopted: no state may be conveyed by colour alone. Đạt carries a check plus the word; chưa đạt carries a cross plus a wavy underline on the failing phoneme; tier-2 warnings carry an exclamation plus a dashed border. Protects red-green colour-blind users, roughly 1 in 12 men, in a product whose entire value is right-versus-wrong feedback
