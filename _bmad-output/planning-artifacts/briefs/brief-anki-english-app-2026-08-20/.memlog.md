- (decision) Pivot from free-form Gemini Live conversation to scripted practice with pronunciation scoring — reference text is known, so assessment is far more accurate and the Live WebSocket/VAD/PCM stack collapses to a simple POST

- (decision) Positioning: a reflex gym, not a conversation partner. Core insight — conversational reflex is built by timed repetition (fluency-development drilling), not free conversation; this makes the scripted approach pedagogically correct rather than a technical compromise
- (decision) Practice ladder: L1 shadow (text + model audio), L2 cold read (text only), L3 cued production (English hidden, Vietnamese meaning + target-word chips). L4 free conversation explicitly parked — it destroys the reference-text accuracy advantage
- (decision) Key-word accuracy is tiered, never a hard block: T1 = deck target vocabulary (must be correct to pass), T2 = Vietnamese-speaker high-risk patterns (final consonants, -s/-ed, clusters — flagged loudly, not blocking), T3 = function words (detail view only)

- (event) Researched the core thesis. Supporting: Nation's Four Strands defines 'fluency development' as working only with already-known language under time pressure, and prescribes >=25% of course time for it — self-learners give it near 0%. Mechanism is faster lexical retrieval on repeat, which is exactly what TTFW measures. 4/3/2 technique shows 86->127 wpm across three deliveries with fewer hesitations
- (event) Counter-evidence found and accepted: transfer of repetition gains to NEW tasks has never been clearly demonstrated; the Audiolingual method failed historically for exactly this reason (pattern mastery without real-world communication); and repetition improves fluency only — accuracy and syntactic complexity stay flat
- (change) DESIGN RULE CHANGE from Suzuki 2022: massed repetition is counterproductive. Six consecutive repeats in one sitting reduced pausing but DEGRADED articulation rate and increased verbatim parroting, still measurable a week later. Limit to 2-3 attempts per sentence per session and bring the sentence back on later days instead. This contradicts the 'repeat until it fires in 2 seconds' assumption in the first draft
- (decision) Spaced re-drilling of sentences is required. This does NOT reverse the no-own-SRS decision — scheduling when a SENTENCE returns for drilling is distinct from competing with Anki on memorization scheduling
- (decision) Reframe why L4 free conversation is parked: not because it lacks value, but because the market already supplies it free (ChatGPT voice). Honest positioning is 'we do the part nobody does; get the rest free elsewhere' rather than pretending drilling alone is sufficient. Nation requires all four strands
- (assumption) Scope honesty for marketing: the product must NOT claim to improve grammar or syntactic complexity. Evidence supports speed/fluency gains plus pronunciation via the scoring layer, nothing more
- (decision) Blind test added to v1 scope: periodically serve a dialogue the user has never seen, built from words they HAVE drilled, and measure TTFW on it. TTFW on drilled sentences always falls even under pure memorisation, so it proves nothing alone — the blind test is what distinguishes real automatisation from parroting, and is the direct check on the transfer risk
- (decision) Spaced re-drill queue also promoted into v1 scope (was implicit) — required by the massed-repetition finding
